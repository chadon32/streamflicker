# StreamFlicker Brand Reference

## Overview

StreamFlicker uses a dark, cinematic discovery interface built around a large featured-movie hero followed by dense horizontal poster collections. Near-black surfaces keep colorful movie artwork dominant, while rose-red controls and gold score badges create a clear action hierarchy. Rounded panels, compact metadata, and provider badges give the product a premium streaming-dashboard feel without losing the energy of a movie poster wall.

## Colors

- **Page Surface**: `#070709` — primary near-black canvas.
- **Panel Surface**: `#121218` — filter bars and larger grouped controls.
- **Elevated Surface**: `#18181B` — cards, pills, and interactive controls.
- **Primary Text**: `#F4F4F5` — titles and high-priority interface copy.
- **Muted Text**: `#9F9FA9` — metadata, descriptions, and secondary labels.
- **Primary Accent**: `#E11D48` — brand rose for major actions and highlights.
- **Bright Accent**: `#EC003F` — vivid CTA fills and active controls.
- **Focus Rose**: `#FB7185` — focused or emphasized interface details.
- **Score Gold**: `#FFB900` — catalog ratings and achievement signals.
- **Provider Green**: `#1CE783` — Hulu/provider availability.

## Typography

- **Display Font**: Outfit (500, 600, 700, 800, 900). Used for the logo wordmark, hero title, section headings, and high-impact promo typography.
- **Interface Font**: Inter (300, 400, 500, 600, 700, 800). Used for body copy, metadata, controls, and provider labels.
- **Hero Scale**: approximately 60px at 900 weight.
- **Section Scale**: approximately 24px at 900 weight.
- **Card Scale**: approximately 16px at 700 weight.
- **UI Scale**: compact 11–16px text with strong weight changes instead of oversized copy.

## Elevation

Depth comes from subtle shifts among `#070709`, `#121218`, and `#18181B`, edged with thin low-contrast borders and generous rounded corners. The primary rose CTA carries a localized bloom, while hero imagery uses a strong black gradient for text readability. Components feel stacked and tactile, but shadows stay restrained so poster art remains the dominant source of color.

## Components

- **Cinematic Feature Hero**: wide rounded panel with full-bleed backdrop, left-weighted title stack, dark image gradient, score badge, metadata, and paired actions.
- **Smart Search Bar**: long pill-shaped command field embedded in the top navigation.
- **Discovery Filter Deck**: dense rounded panel of eras, genres, services, and evidence-based microtags with active rose and gold pills.
- **Poster Rails**: horizontally composed movie-card collections grouped by mood or genre.
- **Catalog Score Badge**: compact gold-on-dark label positioned consistently on hero and poster cards.
- **Movie Poster Cards**: tall artwork, title/metadata footer, provider chips, and compact trailer/watchlist/share actions.
- **Provider Badges**: high-saturation miniature pills using recognizable service colors.
- **Rose Action Buttons**: rounded controls with a play icon, bold white label, and restrained neon bloom.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do's

- Keep the canvas near-black and let poster artwork provide most of the color.
- Use Outfit at heavy weights for concise, cinematic statements.
- Use rose accents for the single most important action in each frame.
- Preserve rounded panels, thin borders, and compact metadata details.
- Use dark gradients over imagery whenever text shares the frame.

### Don'ts

- Do not introduce bright full-frame backgrounds.
- Do not flatten poster artwork into a uniform desaturated treatment.
- Do not use square cards or sharp interface corners.
- Do not fill scenes with long paragraphs or tiny unreadable UI.
- Do not add unrelated gradients, glass effects, or generic streaming-brand motifs.
