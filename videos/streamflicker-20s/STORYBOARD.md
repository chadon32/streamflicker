# StreamFlicker — 20-Second Product Promo Storyboard

**Format:** 1920×1080 landscape, 30 fps, 20 seconds  
**Audio:** HyperFrames TTS voiceover + restrained cinematic electronic underscore + interface SFX  
**VO direction:** Warm, confident adult voice; quick conversational delivery with a slight smile, clean pauses after the hook and before the final wordmark  
**Style basis:** `DESIGN.md` — near-black surfaces, rose action glow, Outfit/Inter typography, vivid poster art, rounded streaming UI  
**Music direction:** Low cinematic synth pulse with soft analog texture. A muted kick establishes momentum after the hook, percussion tightens through discovery and feature beats, then drops into a warm resolving chord for the final brand frame.

## Global Guardrails

- Keep `#070709` present in every beat so the promo feels native to the product.
- Use `#E11D48` or `#EC003F` for the single dominant action in each frame.
- Make poster artwork vivid and dimensional; never flatten it into wallpaper.
- Every beat includes visible camera movement plus at least two depth layers.
- Interface copy must remain legible at 1920×1080 and never compete with narration.
- Use motion that is confident and cinematic: drifts, cascades, whips, draws, and snaps—not generic fades.

## BEAT 1 — THE ENDLESS SCROLL (0.00–3.18s)

**VO:** “Still scrolling for something worth watching?”

**Concept:** The viewer begins inside an endless tunnel of movie choices. Posters fly past with seductive color but no decision, then the StreamFlicker play mark appears like a navigational beacon and arrests the chaos.

**Visual:** A deep `#070709` space holds three curved poster rails at different Z-depths. Thirty-plus captured posters stream horizontally at varied speeds while cropped product screenshots float farther back as enormous, soft-focus catalog planes. A thin rose search path draws through the tunnel. On “watching,” motion rapidly decelerates; Scream 7 centers, the logo resolves above it, and the rose play icon pulses once.

**Mood:** Premium streaming title sequence with the momentum of a theatrical trailer countdown.

**Techniques:** CSS 3D transforms for poster depth; SVG path drawing for the search trail; per-word kinetic typography for the hook.

**Animation choreography:**

- Poster rails RUSH in opposite directions, then BRAKE into alignment.
- Background screenshots DRIFT diagonally at 4–6% scale change.
- Search path DRAWS from left edge toward the hero card.
- Hook words DROP in one by one with decaying horizontal travel.
- Logo play mark STAMPS into place on the final syllable.

**Transition:** Cinematic zoom through the centered Scream 7 poster, 0.45s, `power2.inOut`.

**Depth:** BG screenshot planes and rose particles; MG poster tunnel; FG kinetic hook and logo.

**SFX:** Soft film-reel flutter, quick sequence of browsing ticks, then one low impact as the scroll stops.

## BEAT 2 — CUT THROUGH THE NOISE (3.18–6.11s)

**VO:** “StreamFlicker cuts through the noise. Search by era, genre,”

**Concept:** The apparent chaos reorganizes into useful choices. The product’s filter deck becomes a living control surface, showing that discovery is deliberate rather than random.

**Visual:** `scroll-012.png` enters as a perspective-tilted interface slab. The filter deck is recreated larger in the foreground with era pills, genre pills, and the smart search field. A rose cursor traces a deliberate path from search to “2020s,” then “Sci-Fi.” Poster cards behind the panel instantly reshuffle into a focused selection. Small score badges count up toward 9.9.

**Mood:** Precise, satisfying, tactile—like a premium editing console snapping into focus.

**Techniques:** CSS 3D product showcase; GSAP MotionPath for the cursor; counter animation; SVG connector drawing.

**Animation choreography:**

- Screenshot slab TILTS from 7° to 0° and SETTLES.
- Search query TYPES on as “mind-bending sci-fi.”
- Cursor GLIDES along a rose SVG route.
- Filter pills CASCADE in, then selected pills FILL rose and gold.
- Poster cards SHUFFLE with a staggered 3D flip.
- Score numbers COUNT UP and lock at 9.9.

**Transition:** Velocity-matched upward exit: panel accelerates to `y:-150` with 30px blur over 0.33s; next beat enters from below.

**Depth:** BG catalog screenshot; MG filter panel and poster rail; FG cursor, particles, and score labels.

**SFX:** Search keystrokes, soft pill clicks, card shuffle whoosh, restrained confirmation chime.

## BEAT 3 — DISCOVER YOUR WAY (6.11–8.76s)

**VO:** “streaming service, or evidence-based tags.”

**Concept:** Discovery expands from broad genres into highly specific intent. Provider badges and evidence-based microtags orbit a central selection, demonstrating breadth without becoming a static feature list.

**Visual:** A hero poster card for Blade Runner 2049 rotates gently in the center. Provider badges—Max, Hulu, Prime Video, Apple TV—arc around it on curved paths. Microtags such as `#MindBending`, `#Cyberpunk`, `#SpaceExploration`, and `#SciFiHorror` snap into an organized constellation. Crops from `scroll-049.png` and `scroll-061.png` slide behind as catalog evidence.

**Mood:** Cinematic data visualization: useful, vivid, and controlled.

**Techniques:** MotionPath badge orbits; Canvas 2D rose particle field; CSS 3D card rotation; per-word typography.

**Animation choreography:**

- Central poster ROTATES from edge-on to face camera.
- Provider badges ORBIT and DOCK beneath the card.
- Microtags SHOOT in from the edges and SNAP to a radial grid.
- Background screenshots WHIP past at different parallax speeds.
- Narration keywords PUNCH on in Outfit 900 and recede into interface labels.

**Transition:** Hard cut on a trailer play-button click.

**Depth:** BG screenshot strips and particle haze; MG provider/tag constellation; FG poster card and play button.

**SFX:** Airy orbit swishes, magnetic snaps, and one crisp play click.

## BEAT 4 — FROM DISCOVERY TO MOVIE NIGHT (8.76–12.52s)

**VO:** “Preview trailers, build your watchlist, and check availability in one place.”

**Concept:** The promo converts browsing into action. Three product actions happen in one continuous camera move, proving StreamFlicker is a decision surface rather than another passive catalog.

**Visual:** Camera lands inside the `scroll-000.png` hero. The rose “Watch Trailer” button blooms and a cinematic frame expands from it. The frame slides aside as a bookmark icon draws itself and the Scream 7 card drops into a watchlist stack. Finally, Max and Hulu availability badges rise from the hero’s lower-right corner while a thin line connects title, watchlist, and providers.

**Mood:** Decisive and rewarding; the UI responds immediately to intent.

**Techniques:** SVG icon/path drawing; CSS 3D UI expansion; velocity-matched camera move; kinetic typography synced to action verbs.

**Animation choreography:**

- Hero screenshot PUSHES from 96% to 104% scale.
- Trailer button BLOOMS and EXPANDS into a floating player frame.
- Bookmark outline DRAWS, FILLS rose, and DROPS the card into a stacked watchlist.
- Provider badges RISE with elastic restraint and lock to the connecting path.
- “PREVIEW / SAVE / STREAM” words STAMP in as each action completes.

**Transition:** Blur-through into the final logo, 0.3s out and 0.25s in.

**Depth:** BG Scream 7 backdrop; MG product hero and player/watchlist cards; FG drawn connector, action words, and provider badges.

**SFX:** Trailer bass swell, bookmark tick, two provider badge pops, then music drops to a held chord.

## BEAT 5 — BRAND RESOLVE (12.52–20.00s)

**VO:** “Your next movie night starts here. StreamFlicker.”

**Concept:** Everything converges into one invitation. Poster fragments fold into the play mark, revealing a clean brand frame with enough silence for the name to land.

**Visual:** Eight poster tiles sweep inward and tile briefly into a rose play-button silhouette. The real `logo-f9310505.svg` resolves at center over a soft radial rose glow. Beneath it, “INSTANT TRAILERS & STREAMING” tracks on in Inter, followed by a single rose pill: “Find what to watch.” Fine gold score particles settle around the mark, then become still for the final half-second.

**Mood:** Confident theatrical end card; simple, premium, memorable.

**Techniques:** CSS 3D tile fold; SVG logo reveal; per-word kinetic typography; audio-reactive glow.

**Animation choreography:**

- Poster tiles SWEEP inward and FOLD into the play silhouette.
- Logo SVG REVEALS from the play mark outward.
- Tagline TRACKS on letter-by-letter.
- CTA pill FILLS from left to right in `#EC003F`.
- Rose glow BREATHES once with the final music chord, then holds.

**Transition:** None; hold the completed end card for the final 0.5s.

**Depth:** BG radial glow and subtle grain; MG poster-tile silhouette; FG logo, tagline, and CTA.

**SFX:** Warm logo impact, clean two-note resolve, half-second of stable tail.
